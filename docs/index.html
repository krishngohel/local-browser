How it works

Four steps to a connected browser

01

Install & leave it running

Echo lives in your tray (Windows/Linux) or menu bar (Mac). Closing the window just hides it.

02

Connect your AI app

Open Settings → Connections and click Connect for Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT/Codex, or any MCP client.

03

Restart it

Fully quit and reopen that app so it picks up Echo — not just the chat window.

04

Ask it to browse

Tell it to search the web or open a page. It drives the window you see, with your own login.
