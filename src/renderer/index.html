Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

Echo runs on your computer. It does not create an account with us and does not upload your browsing to our servers.

What stays on your device

  • Cookies and site storage for this app only — not your Google Chrome profile
  • Downloads, screenshots, recordings, and test reports in the app data folder (Settings → System)
  • A local MCP token so only clients you configure can control the browser. The server also listens on your local network.

What other companies may see

Sites you visit (including Google Search) receive your request, IP address, and cookies under their policies.

If you connect Cursor, Claude Desktop, or another MCP client, that app can see page content, screenshots, and anything you are signed in to here. Their privacy policies apply to that data.

  • If you turn on Sessions and state, an assistant can read the cookies and storage for sites you are signed in to, including sign-in tokens, and clear that data

What we do not collect

We do not run an account system, ads, or our own analytics. We do not sell browsing data.

Your choices

  • Skip Connect if you do not want an assistant to see this window
  • Sign out of sites you do not want shared with an assistant
  • Delete the app data folder to wipe the local profile and token
